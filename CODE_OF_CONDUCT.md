# Code of Conduct — SecureScan Pro X

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting
- Any form of offensive, harmful, or discriminatory language
- Promoting or sharing offensive content
- Advocating for unauthorized access to systems

## Security-Specific Standards

As a security tool, we hold ourselves to additional standards:

- **Never** share vulnerabilities without authorization
- **Never** promote offensive use of defensive tools
- **Always** encourage responsible disclosure
- **Always** respect system boundaries and authorization
- **Always** prioritize defensive security

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Consequences may include:
1. Warning
2. Temporary ban
3. Permanent ban

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to conduct@securescan.dev.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
